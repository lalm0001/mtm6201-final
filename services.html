How We Help Animals

Every animal deserves a second chance. ANM Compassion offers a range of programs designed to rescue, rehabilitate, and rehome animals across the Ottawa region.

Adopt Donate

Our Programs

Pet Adoption

We carefully match animals with families, ensuring compatibility through meet-and-greets and a thorough but simple application process.

Medical Support

Every rescued animal receives a full veterinary check-up, vaccinations, spay or neuter surgery, and any required medical treatment.

Foster Care

Our foster program provides a warm, temporary home for animals recovering from illness or waiting for their perfect match.

Rehabilitation

We work with certified animal behaviourists to rehabilitate traumatised animals, preparing them for a happy and settled home life.

Volunteering

Join hundreds of dedicated volunteers who walk dogs, socialise cats, assist at events, and help change lives every week.

Community Education

We host workshops and school visits to promote responsible pet ownership, animal welfare awareness, and compassion in our community.

Donations

Every dollar donated directly funds shelter operations, food, veterinary care, and rescue missions for animals in critical need.

Rescue Transport

Our volunteer transport network ensures animals from remote or high-risk areas reach our shelter safely and without delay.
