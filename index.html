How Adoption Works

1. Select a Companion

Browse our available pets online or visit our Ottawa shelter to meet your future family member in person.

2. Apply & Visit

Complete our simple application form and schedule a meet-and-greet to make sure it is a perfect match for everyone.

3. Welcome Home

Finalise the adoption, receive a starter kit, and bring your new best friend home to start a lifetime of love.
